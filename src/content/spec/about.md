# About
Tabination is an open initiative to provide guitar tabs for songs that are hard to find online. The goal is to help guitarists learn and play music that isn’t well-covered elsewhere.

I’ve always wanted to play guitar leads, but I could never find tabs for the songs I wanted to learn. As I practiced, I began creating my own tabs and decided to share them with others who might be in the same situation.

> [!NOTE]
> 
> I'm also developing a feature in which users will also be able to submit their own tabs for review, and once approved, their tabs will be featured on the site.
