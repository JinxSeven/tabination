# Tabination
[Tabination](https://jinxseven.github.io/tabination/) is an open initiative to provide guitar tabs for songs that are hard to find online. The goal is to help guitarists learn and play music that isn’t well-covered elsewhere.

## Purpose
I’ve always wanted to play guitar leads, but I could never find tabs for the songs I wanted to learn. As I practiced, I began creating my own tabs and decided to share them with others who might be in the same situation.

## Feature Plan
The platform will soon allow users to:

- Submit their own tabs for review
- Have approved tabs published and featured on the site
- Explore a growing community-driven collection of guitar tabs
